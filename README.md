#Attendence Tracker
📚 Attendance Trackie

A student-friendly Android attendance tracker designed to simplify attendance management and academic planning.

✨ Features

- 📊 Subject-Wise Attendance: Track present, absent, and holiday records for individual subjects, including regular classes and labs.
- - 🎯 Custom Percentage Tracker: Personalize how your attendance score is calculated by setting a starting percentage, choosing how many percentage points each Present class adds, and deciding how many points each Absent class deducts. Configure independent scoring rules for regular subjects and laboratory sessions, monitor your score as you record attendance, and use the Bunk Calculator to estimate how future absences could affect it. Corrected attendance records are reflected in recalculated scores, helping you experiment with different tracking settings without permanently accumulating duplicate changes. Your score is based on your chosen rules, giving you greater flexibility than a conventional attendance-percentage formula.

    
- 📅 Weekly Timetable: Organize classes by day and period, with subject, class type, and schedule details.
- 🗓️ Semester Management: Configure semester dates and plan around the academic calendar.
- 🧮 Bunk Calculator: Estimate how future absences may affect your score using the remaining scheduled classes.
- 📈 Reports & Statistics: Review overall attendance, present and absent class counts, and subject-wise progress.
- 🌙 Dark Mode: Switch to a darker interface for a different viewing experience.
- 🎨 Character-Inspired UI: Enjoy a clean, distinctive interface with character illustrations and visual reactions.
- 💾 Attendance History: Maintain and review recorded attendance, with corrections reflected in your calculations.
